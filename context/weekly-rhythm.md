# Weekly rhythm

## Mornings (every weekday)
- 6:50 wake up → coffee + shower → check in with the harness
- 7:20 leave, driving to work (~40 min)
- 8:00–5:00 work. No school work fits in the morning or during the workday.

## School blocks
| Day | School block | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | ~7:00–11:00 PM | After boxing (5:30–6:30). Last night before the deadline. |
| Tue | none | Class night. Assignment due 5:30 PM, before class. |
| Wed | evening, shorter | Weeknight. Stop by 11:00 PM at the latest. |
| Thu | evening, shorter | Weeknight. Stop by 11:00 PM at the latest. |
| Fri | none | Off. Social night. |
| Sat | ~12:00 to late afternoon | After boxing (9:00–10:15). Main block. |
| Sun | ~12:00 to late afternoon | After boxing (10:30–11:45). Main block. |

- **Hard cutoff:** 11:00 PM on any night. Never plan school work past it.
- Weeknight sessions are shorter than weekend ones. Don't plan a whole step on a weeknight unless it's small.
- Leave time to eat and get home: work ends at 5, so a weeknight block realistically starts around 6:00–6:30 (7:00 on Mondays).

## Fixed weekly events (also on the calendar)
- Work: Mon–Fri 8:00–5:00
- Boxing: Mon 5:30 PM, Sat 9:00 AM, Sun 10:30 AM
- Class: Tue evening (MPCS 51238)
